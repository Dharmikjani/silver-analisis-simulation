## 2. Metallization Silver Consumption Intensity ($\text{mg/Wp}$)

Perovskite-silicon tandem cells stack a high-bandgap perovskite top cell on top of a low-bandgap silicon bottom cell (TOPCon or HJT base). This dual-cell architecture requires specialized low-temperature metallization on both sides to avoid damaging the thermally sensitive perovskite layer (temperature ceiling $<140^\circ\text{C}$).

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| SOLAR CELL METALLIZATION SILVER INTENSITY COMPARISON                                                                  |
| Technology Generation    | Metallization Type            | Silver Loading ($\text{mg/Wp}$) | Processing Temp Ceiling|
|--------------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------|
| **Standard PERC**        | High-Temp Screen Print (Ag)   | 10.5 mg/Wp                       | >800°C                 |
| **TOPCon (N-type)**      | Dual-sided Ag-Al Paste        | 13.8 mg/Wp                       | 750°C - 800°C          |
| **HJT (Heterojunction)** | Low-Temp Ag Paste             | 21.5 mg/Wp                       | <200°C                 |
| **Perovskite Tandem (Unoptimized)**| Low-Temp Ag Curing  | **22.5 - 25.0 mg/Wp**            | <140°C                 |
| **Perovskite Tandem (Advanced)**| AgNW Nanowires + Low-Temp| **14.5 - 18.0 mg/Wp**            | <140°C                 |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 2.1 Metallization Paste Suppliers Market Share
Three dominant suppliers control over 85% of the global solar metallization paste and silver nanowire (AgNW) market:
1. **DKEM (Zhejiang Dajia New Material)** — 38% market share (Leading supplier of N-type TOPCon & low-temp tandem pastes).
2. **Heraeus Photovoltaics** — 28% market share (Leading European/Asian low-temperature silver paste developer).
3. **Fusion New Material / Solamet (DuPont spinoff)** — 22% market share (High-efficiency screen printing pastes).

## 4. Quantitative Impact on Free Float & Silver Spot Prices

1. **Free Float Depletion Acceleration**:
   - Tandem PV commercial adoption increases annual solar silver demand from **198.5 Moz in 2026** to **295.0 Moz by 2030** (+96.5 Moz increase).
   - This expands the annual physical deficit, pulling forward the **Liquid Free Float depletion window by ~8 months** (locking in the Late 2027 – Mid 2028 Supply Crunch).

2. **Direct Price Target Adjustment**:
   - **Base Price Target**: Adds **+$6.50 / oz** to 2030 Base Price (raising target to **$85.00 / oz**).
   - **Bull Squeeze Peak Target**: Adds **+$12.00 / oz** to 2030 Bull Peak (raising target to **$125.00 / oz**).
