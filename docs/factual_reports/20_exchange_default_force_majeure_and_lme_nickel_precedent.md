### The 3 Stages of COMEX Emergency Intervention:

#### Stage 1: The Margin Hike Escalator (2011 Precedent)
* During the April 2011 silver run to $49.80, CME Group raised margin requirements **5 times in 8 trading days** (increasing cash requirements by 84%).
* This forced smaller hedge funds and retail longs to liquidate, causing an artificial 30% price drop in 96 hours without a single ounce of new physical supply entering the market.

#### Stage 2: "Liquidation-Only" Rule (1980 Hunt Brothers "Silver Rule 7")
* Under COMEX Rule 501, the exchange can declare that trading is restricted to **closing existing positions only**.
* Market participants are forbidden from entering new buy orders, while short banks are allowed to buy back contracts at suppressed prices.

#### Stage 3: Rule 701 — Force Majeure & Mandatory Cash Settlement
* **Rule 701 Text**: If the exchange determines that physical delivery cannot be completed due to market emergencies or physical unavailability, the President/Board may:
  1. Alter delivery schedules.
  2. Order contracts settled in **Cash Only** at a price determined arbitrarily by the exchange clearing committee.
* **The Reality**: Long contract holders who demanded physical silver will simply receive a wire transfer of US Dollars at a fixed artificial rate (e.g., $60–$80/oz), while the physical vaults remain closed.
