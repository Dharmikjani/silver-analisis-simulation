## 3. Real-World Physical Resistance Mechanics (What Moderates the Spike?)

While unconstrained paper defaults could theoretically drive short-term quotes higher, 3 physical friction forces act as high-level resistance bands:

1. **Scrap Processing Refinery Lag (Module F)**: At prices above $100–$150/oz, secondary scrap recycling surges to its physical processing limit of **275 Moz/year**. However, melting and refining scrap into 999 fine bullion bars requires 3 to 6 months of processing time.
2. **Industrial Substitution Capital Implementation Lag (Modules B & G)**: At $70–$100/oz, solar panel manufacturers accelerate copper electroplating trials. Installing new copper plating lines requires **12 to 18 months of factory CAPEX deployment**, meaning solar makers remain forced to buy silver at high spot prices during the initial 1-2 years of a squeeze.
3. **Exchange Emergency Measures**: COMEX and LBMA raise margin requirements to 100% cash and restrict trades to "Liquidation Only", creating temporary price pullbacks after squeeze spikes.
